# Home (portfolio) surface brief

Scope: the home route and the shared chrome (nav, footer, AI-twin launcher); inner pages inherit the world. Mode: Experience (the work leads), with Persuade's one clear action (contact / résumé).
Audience and job: see PRODUCT.md. Proof: résumé facts only; AMRIP visuals use a labelled synthetic sample.
Pinned by the user: noir + oxblood luxury direction; signature hero = prescription → structured data. Build path: code-led (no image generation).
Unresolved: real project media (none on hand), custom domain.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A private dossier, not a developer template. The page proves one mechanism at full scale — a handwritten prescription read, verified and rewritten as structured data — and refuses the category default of slogan + avatar + metric cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm noir ground (near-black with brown undertone), ivory type, one oxblood seal used at most once per viewport. High-contrast didone display, quiet grotesque body, small caps for labels. Hairline ivory rules instead of boxes; no cards, no side stripes, no eyebrows, no section numbers, no gradient orbs.

STORY: In seconds the visitor sees who Sarthak is and watches his flagship work happen; scrolling, they find measured proof (projects, research), then one obvious way to reach him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed WebGL field. A synthetic prescription of ivory ink particles sits centre-right; an oxblood scan line reads it, fields lock in, and the ink re-forms as a FHIR record; the pointer disturbs the ink. "Sarthak Kar" in the didone spans the lower left at display scale; one line of positioning and the résumé + contact actions sit beside it; a small caption labels the sample synthetic and links the case study.

FORM: Pinned by the user (noir dossier + signature data-morph hero); concept roll not run. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
